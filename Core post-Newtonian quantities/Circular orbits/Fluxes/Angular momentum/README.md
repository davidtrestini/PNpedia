# Conventions

## Orbital frequency vs waveform frequency

There are also two notions of frequency: (i) the orbital frequency $\omega$, and (ii) the waveform frequency $\omega_{22}$, defined here as the half-frequency of the (2,2) mode. These frequencies typically enter expressions through the dimensionless variables $x=(G m \omega/c^3)^{2/3}$ and $x_{22}=(G m \omega_{22}/c^3)^{2/3}$, which also count PN order. There differ by small 4PN terms : $x_{22} = x (1+\mathcal{O}(x^4))$

## Fluxes in terms of orbital and waveform frequency

When expressed in terms of the orbital frequency $x$, the fluxes depend on an arbitrary scale $b_0$, which is related to the choice of slicing in relating near-zone and far zone quantities. Conversely, when expressed in terms of the waveform frequency $x_{22}$, the fluxes do not depend on the scale $b_0$.

## What is presented

In the section ``In terms of orbital frequency'', we provide:
* the fluxes of angular momentum through future null infinity and the horizons in terms of orbital frequency, $F_\text{J}^\infty(x)$ and $F_\text{J}^{\mathcal{H}_{12}}(x)$ [depends on $b_0$]
In the section ``In terms of waveform frequency'', we provide:
* the fluxes of angular momentum through future null infinity and the horizons in terms of waveform frequency, $F_\text{J}^\infty(x_{22})$ and $F_\text{J}^{\mathcal{H}_{12}}(x_{22})$ [does not depend on $b_0$]
